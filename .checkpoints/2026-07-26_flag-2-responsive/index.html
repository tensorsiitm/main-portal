[ WHO WE ARE ]

A student-run consultancy that ships real products — and gives back.

Tensors began as an NGO at IIT Madras and grew into a full-fledged tech consultancy run entirely by students. We take on real client briefs, design and build software end-to-end, and reinvest what we learn into social impact and mentorship.

[ WHAT DRIVES US ]

Our Values

The principles behind every brief we take on.

01

Learning

Every brief is a chance to learn a new stack, domain, or way of working — in public, on real deadlines.

02

Mentorship

Seniors guide juniors through their first real client, first deploy, first hard conversation.

03

Social Impact

We choose work that helps people — NGOs, health, education — not just work that pays.

04

Skill Development

We turn coursework into shipped products, and students into engineers, designers, and leads.

05

Ownership

Every brief is run like it's our own company on the line — deadlines, quality, and follow-through, no excuses.

06

Innovation

We reach for the best tool for the job, not just the familiar one, and aren't afraid to prototype something new.

07

Collaboration

Three verticals, one team — Tenlink, Tenscii and Tenment work in lockstep on every brief.

08

Client Focus

We build for the people who'll actually use it — briefs are judged by real outcomes, not just deliverables.
