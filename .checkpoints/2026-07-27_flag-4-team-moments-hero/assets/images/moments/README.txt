MOMENTS PHOTOS — drop-in instructions
=====================================

The Moments strip auto-loads up to 15 photos from THIS folder. No code changes
needed — just save your photos here with these exact names:

  moment-01.jpg
  moment-02.jpg
  moment-03.jpg
   ... through ...
  moment-15.jpg

Notes:
- Format: .jpg   (lowercase names, zero-padded: moment-01, not moment-1)
- Any shape works — each is auto-cropped to a uniform slide (object-fit: cover).
- IMPORTANT: resize before adding. Phone photos are ~4000px / several MB each;
  15 of those will make the page slow. Export each to about 1200px on the long
  edge (~200-400 KB). That's plenty sharp for the strip.
- Fewer than 15 is fine — any missing number just falls back to a placeholder.
- The order is randomised on every page load, so you don't need to sequence them.

After adding files, refresh the page — they appear in the moving strip.
